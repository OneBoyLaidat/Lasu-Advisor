# LASU Advisor - Academic Performance & Advising System

An academic performance tracker and advising enterprise platform tailored for Lagos State University (LASU). The system standardizes academic audits, tracks student progress under the Nigerian Universities Commission (NUC) 5.0 grading scale, and enables targeted advising interventions across faculties and departments.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🎓 Student Academic Dashboard
- **Cumulative & Semester CGPA Tracking**: Real-time metric computations against official NUC 5.0 grade points.
- **Transcript & Result OCR Scanner**: Automatic extraction and parsing of official LASU portal result slips and PDF transcripts.
- **Advising & Appointment Booking**: Direct booking system with designated departmental Level Advisers.
- **Academic Progress Ring**: Visual breakdown of completed units, registered credits, and carryover flags.

### 2. 👨‍🏫 Level Adviser & Lecturer Portal
- **Advisee Roster Management**: Comprehensive student cohort oversight with compliance status and academic standing filters.
- **Early Warning & At-Risk Intervention**: Instant detection of students below 2.50 CGPA or with carryover deficiencies.
- **One-Click Nudge System**: Dispatch official result submission reminders and schedule advising sessions.
- **Today's Agenda**: Real-time advising session scheduler with reminder toggles.

### 3. 🏛️ Faculty Executive Overview & Macro Analytics
- **NUC Benchmark Distribution**: Macro-level performance distribution (First Class, Second Class Upper/Lower, Third Class, Pass/Fail).
- **Faculty & Departmental Breakdown**: Comparative performance analytics across faculties and academic departments.
- **Accreditation & Audit Reporting**: Instant exportable academic audit reports.

### 4. 🎨 Design & Experience
- **Default High-Contrast Dark Mode**: Polished dark theme by default with light/dark/system toggle support.
- **LASU Brand Identity**: Official university insignia, emblems, and Lagos State University color palette.
- **Responsive Architecture**: Optimized for desktop workstations, tablets, and mobile screens.
